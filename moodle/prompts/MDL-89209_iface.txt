Issue MDL-89209: course_delete_modules is no longer idempotent when the course module record is missing

h2. Background

Prior to [https://moodle.atlassian.net/browse/MDL-86856|https://moodle.atlassian.net/browse/MDL-86856|smart-link],  {{course_delete_module}} checked whether the *course_modules* record existed and returned successfully when it did not. This made repeated execution harmless.

After the refactor, {{course_delete_modules::execute}} now first calls {{context_module::instance($cm->id)->get_course_context()}} which throws an {{Invalid course module ID}} exception when the record is missing.
