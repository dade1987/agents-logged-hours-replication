Issue MDL-87201: Remove subsections from the recycle bin

After reviewing [https://moodle.atlassian.net/browse/MDL-80194|https://moodle.atlassian.net/browse/MDL-80194|smart-link], we are removing subsections from the recycle bin. This change avoids confusion and aligns their behaviour with sections, which also do not appear in the bin:

While a more comprehensive solution would involve changing the section behaviour as well, that would require significantly more effort than is currently available. We are therefore opting for this simpler, more consistent approach.



*Steps to reproduce it*

# Create a course.
# Add a subsection with, at least, one activity.
# Remove the subsection.
# Run the cron.
# Go to Course > More > Recycle bin.

Only the activities in the subsection are displayed in the Recycle bin.



*Current behaviour*
